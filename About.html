About FOXTROT

PPV FOXTROT (Personal Portfolio Venture) is a personal engineering and technology portfolio created to document, organize, and present original projects, research, software, and technical concepts. The website serves as a central archive where ideas can be recorded, developed, and shared in a structured and accessible manner.

FOXTROT is the codename of the portfolio and represents the identity of this independent engineering archive. The purpose of the website is to showcase projects ranging from software applications and electronic devices to theoretical engineering concepts and research studies. The catalog is intentionally designed with a simple and lightweight interface, allowing visitors to navigate efficiently while maintaining an organized directory of projects and their development progress.

About the Developer

PPV FOXTROT was created and is maintained by Pragyan Kalita, a Class IX student from Assam, India, studying at Kendriya Vidyalaya. The portfolio was developed independently out of a strong personal interest in engineering, robotics, software development, aerospace, and scientific research. It exists primarily as a learning platform, personal archive, and long-term record of technical growth.

Every project presented within this portfolio reflects a stage of learning and experimentation. Many concepts are theoretical or under active development and should be viewed as educational work rather than finished commercial products.

Purpose of the Website

The primary objectives of PPV FOXTROT are to:

  • Document engineering concepts and technical research.
  • Archive completed and ongoing projects.
  • Track project development and future improvements.
  • Develop technical documentation and design skills.
  • Create a professional portfolio for future academic and career opportunities.

Access Information

If you are viewing this website, you are likely one of the following:

  • A visitor exploring the public sections of the portfolio.
  • A privileged user who has been granted access to additional project documentation.
  • An educator, collaborator, or recruiter reviewing the author's work and technical abilities.

Access to certain documents or project files may be limited depending on their development status or confidentiality.

Site Guidelines

To maintain a respectful and constructive environment, all visitors are expected to follow these general principles:

  • Respect all users, creators, and published content.
  • Do not copy, redistribute, or claim original work without permission or proper attribution.
  • Use the information provided for educational, research, or personal learning purposes.
  • Some projects may involve engineering concepts related to safety, defense, or protective technologies. These are presented solely for educational and research purposes and must never be applied in illegal, harmful, or unethical ways.
  • Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or offensive behavior based on race, nationality, religion, gender, disability, age, or any other personal characteristic is not tolerated.
  • Visitors are encouraged to maintain respectful discussions and exercise good judgment when interacting with the website or its content.

These guidelines may be revised over time as the portfolio continues to grow and new features are introduced.
